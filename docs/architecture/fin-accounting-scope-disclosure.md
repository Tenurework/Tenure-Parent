## A. Accounting basis

**No accounting basis is declared anywhere in this platform.** There is no basis field, enum or constant: not accrual, not cash, not modified accrual, and no framework (GAAP, IFRS) is named or implemented. Postings are recorded in one unnamed basis, and no statement produced from them may be described as prepared under any standard.

## B. Ledgers, books, currencies and valuation

- Money is exact integer minor units with a per-currency exponent and a caller-stated rounding mode: **yes** (`packages/finops/src/money.ts`). No amount in the platform packages is a float.
- Currency travels on the row in 7 Prisma model(s): `Budget`, `BudgetLine`, `LedgerEntry`, `PaymentsFundsFlowConfig`, `ProviderBalanceTransaction`, `ReceiptAllocation`, `Settlement`.
- A trial balance is produced per currency and never totalled across them: **yes**.
- Conversion at a rate carrying its own date: **available as a function** (`convert`, `packages/finops/src/settlement-components.ts`).
- A STORED exchange rate: **none**. With no rate record there is no revaluation, no translation, no realized/unrealized gain-or-loss and no reporting currency: a caller must supply every rate itself, and nothing reproduces the rate a past posting used.
- Secondary ledgers, books, accounting bases and reporting currencies: **none**. There is one set of postings and no ledger record to attribute them to.

## C. Jurisdiction, tax and statutory reporting

- Tax determination: **none**. No rate is looked up, no rule is applied, no recoverability is decided.
- Tax accounts that exist: `RECOVERABLE_TAX_ACCOUNT` = `1400-recoverable-tax` — a tax amount SUPPLIED BY THE CALLER is posted there by a template revision. That is posting, not determination.
- E-invoicing and statutory transmission: **none**. Nothing is generated, validated, transmitted, accepted or amended.
- Where this platform says "jurisdiction" it means provider country, data residency or a pricing region — see `packages/payments/src/capability-registry.ts` and `packages/provisioning/src/catalogs.ts`. Neither is a tax jurisdiction, and nothing exports a jurisdiction pack. **No tax jurisdiction is supported, in any country.**
