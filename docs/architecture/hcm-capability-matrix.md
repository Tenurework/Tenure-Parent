## 6. Limitations

Stated as limitations because §16 requires unbuilt scope to remain unavailable and
§17 forbids claiming otherwise.

1. **37 of the 45 canonical objects §4 requires do not exist.**
   `Job`, `JobFamily`, `Grade`, `GradeRate`, `Location`, `WorkPattern`, `CollectiveAgreement`, `Worker`, `EmploymentRelationship`, `WorkforceEvent`, `Candidate`, `Requisition`, `Application`, `Interview`, `Offer`, `OnboardingJourney`, `TimeEntry`, `TimeCard`, `Shift`, `AbsencePlan`, `AbsenceBalance`, `LeaveRequest`, `CompensationElement`, `CompensationCycle`, `BenefitPlan`, `Enrollment`, `Goal`, `Review`, `Feedback`, `Skill`, `Profile`, `LearningItem`, `LearningAssignment`, `HRCase`, `PayrollRelationship`, `PayrollInput`, `PayrollRunReference`.
2. **No payroll, benefits, recruiting, time or learning capability is available in
   any jurisdiction**, and none may be enabled without the objects, a provider and a
   certification decision.
3. **No jurisdiction pack exists**, so no local legal requirement is enforced
   anywhere and nothing in the product should read as supporting a country.
4. **The person model is split and the assignment model is doubled** —
   `DirectoryPerson`/`User` joined by email, `RoleAssignment` and `SeatHolding`
   answering one question two ways. Recorded in
   `docs/architecture/hcm-people-inventory.md` §5; blocked on the schema decision
   `HCM-000-002` names.
5. **What IS enforced, and is the only thing this matrix reports as built:** the
   seat-memory boundary (`HCM-040-003`) — a successor inherits the seat's working
   record, never its credentials or anything classified above `standard`.
